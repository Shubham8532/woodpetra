Who We Are

Built on Craft,
Driven by Style

Shubham Fashion was born from a simple belief: everyday clothing should feel exceptional. Founded in 2018 in the heart of Mumbai, we set out to create a fashion brand that blends modern design sensibility with the comfort and durability that real life demands.

What started as a small collection of premium cotton tees quickly grew into a full wardrobe ecosystem — spanning shirts, jeans, hoodies, joggers, and more. Every piece in our catalogue is thoughtfully designed, ethically sourced, and rigorously tested before it reaches you.

Today, Shubham Fashion serves over 50,000 customers across India, with a product range of 100+ styles and counting. We remain committed to our founding promise: delivering fashion that makes you look and feel your best — every single day.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make at Shubham Fashion.

01

Uncompromising Quality

We use only premium, certified fabrics. Every garment undergoes rigorous quality checks before it leaves our warehouse. If it doesn't meet our standards, it doesn't reach you.

02

Inclusive Style

Fashion has no gender. Our collections are designed for everyone — celebrating individuality and self-expression across all body types, sizes, and identities.

03

Sustainable Practices

From eco-conscious packaging to carbon-neutral shipping options, we're committed to reducing our environmental footprint at every stage of the supply chain.

04

Transparent Pricing

Premium fashion shouldn't come with hidden costs. Our pricing reflects real value — quality you can feel without the markup you resent.

05

Community Driven

Our customers inspire our collections. We listen, iterate, and co-create with the community that wears Shubham Fashion every day.

06

Customer Delight

Every interaction — from browsing to delivery to returns — is designed to exceed your expectations. Your smile is our best quality metric.
