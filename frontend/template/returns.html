Returns & Refunds Policy

We want you to love every item you order from Shubham Fashion. If for any reason you're not completely satisfied, our returns process is designed to be simple, transparent, and stress-free.
Last updated: January 2026

1 Overview

Shubham Fashion accepts returns on most items within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be returned in their original, unworn, unwashed condition with all tags attached and in the original packaging.

✅ Our Guarantee If you're not completely happy with your purchase — for any reason — we will accept the return and process a full refund. No questions asked within the 30-day window.

2 Eligible Items for Return

Items eligible for return must meet the following conditions:

  • Returned within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • Item is unworn, unwashed, and unaltered.
  • All original tags and labels are still attached.
  • Item is in its original packaging (box, polybag, or garment bag).
  • Item was purchased directly from shubhamfashion.in or our authorised partners.

3 Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned for hygiene, safety, or customisation reasons:

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered in any way.
  • Items with missing tags, labels, or original packaging.
  • Items purchased during "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" promotions.
  • Customised or personalised items.
  • Gift cards and digital vouchers.

If you are unsure whether an item is eligible, please contact our support team before initiating a return.

4 How to Initiate a Return

Starting a return is quick and easy — just follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Email us at returns@shubhamfashion.in with your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason for return.
  • Step 2: Our team will confirm your return request within 24 hours and send you a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number.
  • Step 3: Schedule a free pickup from your address or drop the parcel at any of our partner courier locations.
  • Step 4: Pack the item securely with the RMA number written on the outside of the package.
  • Step 5: Our team will inspect the item upon receipt and initiate the refund within 2 business days.

5 Refund Timeline

Once your return is received and approved:

  • Refunds are processed within 2 business days of receiving the returned item.
  • Bank transfers and UPI refunds: 3–5 business days.
  • Credit/Debit card refunds: 5–7 business days (depending on your bank).
  • Refund to store credit (optional): Within 24 hours of approval.
💳 Refund Method Refunds are credited to the original payment method used at checkout. If you'd prefer a store credit (which processes faster), let us know when initiating your return.

6 Exchanges

We offer free exchanges for size or colour within the 30-day return window, subject to stock availability. To request an exchange:

  • Follow the same return initiation process (Section 4) and specify that you want an exchange.
  • Let us know the replacement item, size, and colour you'd like.
  • If the replacement is higher in value, you'll pay the difference. If lower, we'll refund the difference.
  • Exchange items are dispatched within 2–3 business days of receiving your return.

7 Defective or Incorrect Items

In the unlikely event that you receive a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery.

  • Photograph the defect or incorrect item clearly.
  • Email us at hello@shubhamfashion.in with the photos and your order ID.
  • We will prioritise your case and arrange a free replacement or full refund within 48–72 hours.
  • You will not be charged any return shipping for defective or incorrect items.

8 Return Shipping

  • We offer free return pickup for orders above ₹999.
  • For orders below ₹999, a ₹49 return shipping fee may be deducted from your refund.
  • All returns for defective, damaged, or incorrect items are fully covered by us at no cost to you.
  • Self-shipped returns must use a trackable shipping method. We are not responsible for lost return parcels.
