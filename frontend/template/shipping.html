Shipping Policy

At Shubham Fashion, we strive to deliver your order as quickly and safely as possible. This policy outlines everything you need to know about our shipping process.
Last updated: January 2026

1 Overview

We process and ship all orders Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next business day. Once your order is confirmed and payment is received, our team begins fulfilment within 1–2 business days.

🚚 Free Shipping Threshold Enjoy free standard shipping on all orders above ₹999. Orders below this amount attract a flat shipping fee of ₹79.

2 Delivery Times

Estimated delivery times from the date of dispatch:

  • Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2–4 business days
  • Tier-2 cities and major towns: 3–5 business days
  • Tier-3 cities and remote areas: 5–8 business days
  • Express delivery (select cities): Next day or same day

Please note that these are estimated timelines. Actual delivery may vary depending on your location, courier capacity, and any unforeseen circumstances.

3 Shipping Charges

Order Value Shipping Fee Estimated Days
Below ₹999 ₹79 2–8 business days
₹999 and above FREE 2–8 business days

All prices include GST. No hidden charges at checkout.

4 Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an email and SMS notification containing your tracking number and a link to track your shipment in real time.

  • Track orders via the courier partner's website using your AWB/tracking number.
  • Tracking updates are available within 24 hours of dispatch.
  • For tracking assistance, email us at hello@shubhamfashion.in with your order ID.

5 Serviceable Areas

Shubham Fashion currently ships to all major cities, towns, and districts across India. We partner with multiple courier services to ensure maximum coverage:

  • All 28 Indian states and 8 Union Territories are covered.
  • Some remote PIN codes may require additional delivery time.
  • International shipping is not yet available but is planned for 2026.

If you're unsure whether we deliver to your location, contact our support team before placing an order.

6 Express Shipping

For customers who need their fashion fix faster, we offer Express Shipping in select metropolitan areas at an additional charge of ₹199.

  • Available in: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata.
  • Orders placed before 12:00 PM are dispatched the same day.
  • Orders placed after 12:00 PM are dispatched the following business day.
  • Express delivery is not available during public holidays or sale events.

7 Delays & Force Majeure

While we make every effort to deliver within the estimated timeframe, delays may occasionally occur due to factors beyond our control, including:

  • Natural disasters, floods, or extreme weather events.
  • Government restrictions, lockdowns, or curfews.
  • Strikes by courier or logistics partners.
  • High-demand periods such as festive sales or holidays.

In the event of significant delays, we will notify you proactively and offer suitable options including rescheduling or a full refund.

8 Damaged or Missing Shipments

If your order arrives damaged or is reported missing, please follow these steps:

  • Take clear photographs of the damaged packaging and product immediately upon receipt.
  • Report the issue to us within 48 hours of delivery via email or phone.
  • We will arrange a replacement or full refund within 5–7 business days after investigation.
  • For missing parcels, we will file a courier investigation — this typically takes 3–5 business days.
⚠️ Important Damaged product claims must be submitted with photographic evidence within 48 hours of delivery. Claims submitted after this window may not be eligible for replacement.
