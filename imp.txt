If you want 99% reliability



Don't make the model infer.



Store structured shopping context.



Example:



last_product = "SummerLite Shorts"



or



last_products = [...]



Then when the user says



What is the price?



you simply do



if (

    intent.intent == IntentType.DETAILS

    and intent.product_name is None

):

    intent.product_name = last_product



Zero hallucination.



Zero ambiguity.



That's how production shopping assistants usually do it.
