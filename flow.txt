🛒 Project Name

AI Shopping Assistant

Objective:
Build an intelligent shopping assistant that understands natural language, extracts shopping intent, searches a product database, remembers conversation context, and recommends products.

This is not a generic chatbot.

It is an AI-powered product search and shopping assistant.

End Goal

A user can ask naturally:

Show me blue cotton T-Shirts under ₹1000

Cheapest jeans

Suggest something for college

Do you have caps?

Only black ones

Another one

What's its price?

Compare the first two.

The assistant understands the request, searches products, and responds naturally.
